# Simi Learn Terms of Use

Effective: September 19, 2026

Simi Learn provides AI-assisted educational explanations. Lessons may contain errors. Students should verify important academic, medical, legal, financial and safety-critical information with a qualified source.

The free plan includes three generated lessons. Student Pro includes up to 30 generated lessons per billing period. Prices and taxes are displayed by Galaxy Store before purchase. Subscriptions renew until cancelled through the Samsung account used to purchase. Restoring a purchase requires that same store account.

Do not use Simi to generate unlawful, abusive or harmful content, attack the service, bypass quotas or expose another person's private information.

The service is provided as available without a guarantee that every topic can be generated. When a lesson fails quality checks, Simi returns an error instead of showing a potentially misleading fallback.
