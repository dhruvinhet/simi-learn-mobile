# Student study protocol

Recruit 10–20 participants aged 13 or older. Obtain informed consent before recording data or quotes. Do not collect school names or sensitive educational records.

Each participant completes one unfamiliar topic:

1. Rate confidence from 1–5.
2. Answer one neutral pre-question.
3. Generate and watch a lesson without coaching.
4. Complete the built-in two-question check.
5. Rate confidence, visual clarity and narration alignment from 1–5.
6. Ask: “Which moment made the idea clearer?” and “What was confusing or missing?”
7. Ask whether they would replay or share the lesson.

Record anonymous participant ID, topic, time-to-first-lesson, completion, quiz score, before and after confidence, clarity, alignment, replay intent and optional consented quote.

Publish aggregate results only. Report sample size and limitations. Never invent testimonials or imply statistical significance from a small convenience sample.
