# Galaxy Store listing draft

## Name

Simi Learn — Visual Explanations

## Short description

Turn a hard topic into a short narrated visual lesson.

## Full description

When words are not enough, make the idea visible.

Type a difficult question, choose your learning level, and Simi Learn builds a short visual explanation with narration, captions and a quick understanding check. Lessons render directly on your Galaxy device and save for replay.

- Native animated diagrams
- Clear on-device narration and captions
- Middle school, high school and college levels
- 45, 60 and 90 second lessons
- Two-question comprehension checks
- Offline replay for saved lessons
- Reduced-motion and narration-speed controls

Simi validates every lesson before playback. If the visual and explanation do not align, Simi asks you to retry instead of showing a generic fallback.

The free plan includes three complete lessons. Student Pro provides up to 30 generated lessons per billing period.
