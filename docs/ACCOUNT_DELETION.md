# Delete a Simi Learn account

Simi Learn supports guest use and optional email sign-in.

## Delete local lessons

Open Simi Learn, open **Library**, remove the lessons you no longer want, or uninstall the app to remove all locally cached lessons and settings.

## Delete a signed-in account and service data

Before store submission, replace SUPPORT_EMAIL with the verified support address.

1. Email **SUPPORT_EMAIL** from the email address used for Simi Learn.
2. Use the subject **Delete my Simi Learn account**.
3. We will verify the request without asking for a password.
4. We will delete the Supabase user and associated generation, feedback, and entitlement records, then confirm completion.

Purchase records that Samsung or RevenueCat must retain for tax, fraud prevention, refunds, or legal compliance follow those providers' retention rules. Deleting a Simi Learn account does not cancel an active Galaxy Store subscription. Cancel it first in Galaxy Store under **Menu > Subscriptions**.
