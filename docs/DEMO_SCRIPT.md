Show a student looking at a dense explanation.

Voice: “When a student cannot picture an idea, another paragraph rarely helps. Simi makes the relationship visible.”

## 0:10–0:25 — The promise

On a real Galaxy device, type “Why do planets orbit the Sun?”, choose middle school and 60 seconds, then tap Create.

Voice: “Ask one hard question and choose the level. Simi plans the complete lesson and validates every visual before playback.”

## 0:25–1:18 — The lesson

Show the real native scenes, on-device narration, captions, pause and scene controls.

Voice: “The planet’s forward motion is shown first. Then gravity changes its direction inward. The final scene combines those relationships into continuous free fall. Narration and visuals share semantic IDs, so an absent or contradictory object cannot pass the quality gate.”

## 1:18–1:42 — Understanding and retention

Answer the two questions, show the result and reopen the lesson from the offline library.

Voice: “A quick check measures whether the idea landed. Validated lessons save locally, so the student can replay them without generating again.”
