# Simi Learn Privacy Policy

Effective: September 19, 2026

Simi Learn helps students create and replay short visual explanations.

## Information processed

When you generate a lesson, Simi processes the topic, selected learner level, duration and language to create the lesson. The topic is sent to our lesson-generation provider. Simi stores the resulting validated lesson and a one-way hash of the normalized topic for reliable retries and abuse prevention.

Simi may store an account identifier, entitlement status, lesson completion, quiz score, generation time and optional rating. Guest lessons are stored on the device. Email is handled by the authentication provider when you choose email sign-in.

## Information not requested

Simi does not request a school name, exact age, contacts, camera, microphone, location, advertising identifier or sensitive education record. It does not sell personal information or use behavioral advertising.

## Purposes

Information is used to create lessons, enforce lesson allowances, restore purchases, operate the app, prevent abuse and measure whether lessons are understandable.

## Providers

Simi uses Supabase for authentication and service storage, Groq for lesson-plan generation, RevenueCat and Galaxy Store for subscription processing, and may use OneSignal for opted-in learning reminders. Each provider processes information under its own terms.

## Retention and deletion

Local lessons remain until deleted or the app is removed. Service records are retained only as needed for operation, security and aggregate product evaluation. Signed-in users may request account and associated service-data deletion at the support address in the Galaxy listing.

## Children

Simi Learn is designed for students aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13.
